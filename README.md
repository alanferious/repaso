# Repaso: IB Spanish ab initio practice

Use it online at **https://alanferious.github.io/repaso/**, or download this folder and open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. It runs offline and needs no installation.

- **Vocabulary**: 25 lists covering the five ab initio themes, their prescribed topics, and core lists (question words, connectives, tense time markers, opinions, numbers, dates).
  Modes: type the answer (English → Spanish, Spanish → English or mixed), multiple choice, and listening.
- **Verb drills**: 94 verbs in 10 tenses (present, preterite, imperfect, near future, future, conditional, present perfect, present continuous, tú imperative, plus present subjunctive as a stretch).
- **Tense guide**: endings, irregular forms, time markers and examples for each tense, plus a conjugator.

Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage). To move it to another device, use **Progress → Copy progress**, then **Paste progress** on the other device.

## Adding words

Edit `vocab.js`. Each line is `spanish | english`:

| Write | Means |
|---|---|
| `el coche; el carro` | either answer is accepted |
| `alto/a` | accepts *alto* or *alta* |
| `el/la profesor/a` | accepts *el profesor* or *la profesora* |
| `el (teléfono) móvil` | the part in brackets is optional |
| `tarde \| late [not early]` | square brackets are a hint and never need typing |

## Notes

The word lists are organised around the ab initio themes and prescribed topics. They are not an official IB list, so check them against your teacher's lists. Not affiliated with the International Baccalaureate.
